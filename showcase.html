OPEN DECISION

Name A/B test before the page goes live

“Banana Airways” is the working name, not the locked one. Before we publish the Steam page we're running a paid A/B test against an alternate candidate — same capsule, same short copy, in front of the actual target audience. Conversion is the tiebreaker.

METHOD

AI heavy in production · zero AI in the final game

We use AI aggressively for concept, refs, prompts, and pipeline work — that's how the visual target landed this fast. But none of it ships in the final game: every shipped asset is hand-modelled, hand-textured, hand-cut. The final product should never feel like AI.

03
GAME LOOP · WIP

Game loop.

Working draft. Calm at the start, problems start to surface, players scramble to fix them, plane survives or falls apart, repeat with worse. Still being designed — pacing, severity curves, and how threats stack on top of each other are open questions.

01

Calm cruise

Cabin is quiet. Passengers ask for coffee. Routine service for the first ~30 seconds.

→
02

First problem

One thing breaks. Small, manageable. The crew has to react now.

→
03

Scramble

Players split. One fixes the threat. Another keeps passengers from rioting. A third checks the next thing.

→
04

Plane survives. Or not.

Crank the difficulty. Next event loads. Problems start to stack.

Threats currently on the list

LOW
Clogged bathroom

Someone has to plunge it. The smell distracts the cabin.

LOW
Bird in the cabin

Catch it before a passenger panics or it hits the cockpit.

LOW
Coffee riot

Unserved passengers escalate from grumpy to throwing things.

MID
Turbulence

Everything not bolted down becomes airborne. Including the cart.

MID
Fuselage hole

Gorilla tape. Plank. Hammer. Before the next passenger gets sucked out.

MID
Broken engine

Rope. Climb out. Tie it back on.

HIGH
Bomb suitcase

Row 14. Visible countdown. Defuse with the wrong wires available.

HIGH
Missile inbound

Cockpit POV. Bank hard.

HIGH
Alien octopus

Sticks to a player's face. Inverts their controls.

06
REVEAL TRAILER · SCRIPT

Reveal trailer script.

ACT 1~5s

Calm

Lockheed Constellation in a wide shot. Soft music. Just the engine.

ACT 2~10–15s

The lying welcome

Inside. A player improvises a flight-attendant voice.

FLIGHT ATTENDANT: “Hello everyone… uh, thank you for flying with us today. We have a, uh… small issue at the back of the plane. But it's fine. Don't worry.”

POV. A passenger turns their head. Gigantic hole. Wind howling.

FLIGHT ATTENDANT (off, still walking): “…enjoy your flight.”

ACT 3~5–8s

Captain · “this is too easy”

A player walks into the captain's cabin. The captain-player is at the controls, relaxed.

CAPTAIN: “Dude, this is way too easy.”

He grabs the yoke and starts swinging it around. The plane lurches — camera shakes, cabin objects fly.

ACT 4~10s

The coffee knockout

Cabin. Player A is surrounded by passengers with thought bubbles begging for coffee.

PLAYER A (yelling): “Dude, can you throw me the coffee?”

PLAYER B: “Yeah!”

B hurls the pot like a fastball. Camera follows. Impact. A drops like a ragdoll. The passengers keep ordering coffee over the body.

ACT 5~10–15s

Captain on the intercom · chaos reveal

Cabin again. The intercom crackles — the captain's voice, distorted.

CAPTAIN (over intercom): “Yo, uh… does anyone know why there are so many red lights on the dashboard?”

The players all look up at once. Camera pans to what they're seeing:

  • Engines on fire out the windows
  • Holes in the fuselage, wind blasting in
  • A column of smoke from somewhere
  • Broken stuff. Fire. Total visual chaos.

Nobody picks up the intercom.

ACT 6~10–15s

The missing helper

PLAYER C: “Yo can you grab something to fix this?”

PLAYER D (off, distant): “Yeah, I got it, I got it.”

Long pause. Faraway noises.

PLAYER C: “…bro? Bro, can you help me?”

Cut: D stumbles down the aisle with an alien octopus stuck to their face, bouncing off seats.

ACT 7~18–20s

“We did it” · the mountain

INT. CABIN. Players gathered in one spot. Around them: holes patched with planks and tape, a smoking suitcase in the corner, the octopus on the floor, engines off outside. Post-storm calm.

Breathing. Someone sits down. Quiet laugh.

PLAYER: “Okay… I think we got everything.”

OTHER PLAYER: “Yeah, we did it.”

Beat. Smiles.

One of them slowly looks up. Eyes forward — toward the cockpit.

PLAYER (quiet, almost to themself): “…wait. Who's flying the plane?”

HARD CUT to:

EXT. WIDE PROFILE OF THE PLANE. Gliding, dead level, straight at a mountain. Static lateral camera.

IMPACT. Plane embeds itself in the mountain. Dust, small fire, pieces falling. Beat. Silence. Just mountain wind. Plane sits lodged in the rock, tilted, leaking a thin trail of smoke.

Title card over it:

BANANA AIRWAYS
Wishlist now on Steam
